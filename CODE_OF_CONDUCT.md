# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This document is our own, and is inspired by the widely used
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming, patient, and respectful, especially toward newcomers.
- Assume good intent; give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for learners.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any other channel — and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to
the maintainers. If you are unsure how to reach a maintainer privately, you may
open an issue asking a maintainer to contact you (without including details), or
use GitHub's report-abuse feature. Reports will be handled discreetly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take any action they
deem appropriate, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from
the project's spaces, for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may face
repercussions decided by the project's leadership.
